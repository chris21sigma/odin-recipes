Chicken Curry

Description

A rich and comforting homemade chicken curry featuring tender chicken pieces and golden potatoes simmered in an aromatic, spiced coconut gravy. Finished with a touch of cream and fresh cilantro, this classic dish offers the perfect balance of warm spices and savory goodness

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or coconut oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Sauté the Aromatics: Heat oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add the chopped onions and sauté until translucent and lightly browned (about 5–7 minutes). Stir in the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for another 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Toast the Spices: Add the curry powder, turmeric, and cumin directly into the onion mixture. Stir constantly for about 30 seconds to let the spices release their flavors.
  3. Brown the Chicken: Add the chicken pieces to the skillet, stirring to coat them thoroughly with the spiced onion mixture. Cook until the chicken is lightly browned on all sides.
  4. Simmer: Pour in the coconut milk and add the diced potatoes. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 20–25 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender and the chicken is fully cooked.
  5. Garnish & Serve: Remove from heat, garnish with fresh chopped cilantro, and serve hot!
