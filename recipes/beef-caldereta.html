Beef Caldereta

Description

Beef Caldereta is a rich, hearty, and popular Filipino stew made with tender chunks of beef slow-cooked in a savory tomato-based sauce. Enhanced with liver spread or liver paste for depth, it is traditionally loaded with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, green peas, and olives, then finished with melted cheese for a creamy, comforting flavor that pairs perfectly with warm white rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs beef chuck or brisket, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup liver spread or liver pate
  • 1 cup beef broth or water
  • 1 large potato, cubed
  • 1 large carrot, cubed
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/2 cup quick-melt cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 1/2 cup black or green olives (optional)
  • 1–2 chili peppers or labuyo (optional, for heat)
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Sauté aromatics and brown the meat: Heat cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté the garlic and onions until softened. Add the beef cubes and cook until browned on all sides.
  2. Simmer the beef: Pour in the tomato sauce and beef broth (or water). Add the bay leaves. Bring to a boil, then cover, lower the heat, and let it simmer for about 1 to 1.5 hours (or until the beef is tender).
  3. Add liver spread: Stir in the liver spread until well incorporated into the sauce to build a rich, thick base.
  4. Cook the vegetables: Add the cubed potatoes and carrots, and let them simmer for about 8 to 10 minutes until slightly tender.
  5. Add peppers and optional ingredients: Stir in the red and green bell peppers, green peas, olives, and chili peppers (if using). Simmer for another 5 minutes.
  6. Melt cheese and season: Add the grated cheddar cheese and stir continuously until melted into the sauce. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. Serve: Remove from heat, transfer to a serving dish, and enjoy hot with steaming white rice.
